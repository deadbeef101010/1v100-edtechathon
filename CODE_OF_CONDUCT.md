# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for educators, students, and developers.

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact. Welcome people of every background and experience level. Do not use harassment, threats, sexualized language, discriminatory language, personal attacks, or the publication of another person's private information.

Keep classroom safety central: never share identifiable student data, real credentials, or recordings without clear authority. Feedback about educational practice should address the work, not the educator or student.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools or a private repository security report when sensitive information is involved.
